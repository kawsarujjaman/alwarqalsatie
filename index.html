Who We Are

One contractor. Every trade. Zero handoffs.

Benchmark Contracting Group was built to remove the coordination burden of hiring separate specialists for every trade on a project.

We run dedicated, trained crews across twelve disciplines — glazing, finishes, MEP, interiors, and building services — so your project moves through design, installation, and ongoing maintenance under one contract and one accountable team. That means fewer subcontractors to manage, fewer scheduling conflicts, and a single point of contact who knows the building end to end.

What We Do

Twelve disciplines. One scope of work.

Every service below is delivered by an in-house crew — not re-sold to a third party — and backed by a maintenance plan once installation is complete.

SVC-01

Aluminum & Glass

Curtain walls, storefronts, railings and glazing — installed, sealed, and serviced to manufacturer spec.

SVC-02

Painting & Decoration

Interior and exterior finishes, protective coatings, and decorative treatments for new and refurbished spaces.

SVC-03

Ornamentation & Engraving

Decorative millwork, signage, and precision engraving for lobbies, facades and feature walls.

SVC-04

Plaster Works

Internal and external plastering, skim coating, and surface preparation to a true, paint-ready finish.

SVC-05

Insulation Installation

Thermal and acoustic insulation for walls, roofs and mechanical systems, matched to project code requirements.

SVC-06

Electromechanical Equipment

Installation and maintenance of electromechanical systems, from pumps and panels to control equipment.

SVC-07

Partitions & False Ceilings

Light gauge partitions, gypsum systems, and suspended ceiling installation for offices and fit-outs.

SVC-08

HVAC & Air Filtration

Air conditioning, ventilation, and filtration systems — installed, balanced, and kept running year-round.

SVC-09

Building & Residential Cleaning

Scheduled and one-off cleaning programs for commercial buildings, common areas and residences.

SVC-10

Sanitary & Plumbing

Water supply, drainage, and sanitary fixture installation, plus responsive repair and maintenance.

SVC-11

Wood Flooring & Carpentry

Engineered and solid wood flooring, joinery, and custom carpentry finished to a durable standard.

SVC-12

Wall & Floor Tiling

Ceramic, porcelain and natural stone tiling for bathrooms, kitchens, lobbies and exterior cladding.
